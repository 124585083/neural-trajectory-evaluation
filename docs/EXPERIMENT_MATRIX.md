The five-session result should therefore be interpreted as a response benchmark. The broader output-space RSA/CKA, GPFA trajectory, response-matching, and temporal-ablation conclusions remain a one-session proof of concept. Hidden-layer RSA/CKA, multi-session trajectory replication, multiple encoding-model seeds, and the magnitude-matched non-temporal damage control are not currently complete.
